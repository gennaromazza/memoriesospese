---
name: Lead queue separate from jobs
description: UX and filtering rule for the admin jobs list.
---

Keep unsigned leads in a separate, selectable Lead queue. The main jobs list and its type counts should contain signed/confirmed work only; entering the Lead queue should show requests ordered by their request activity date and keep lead-specific quote filters available.

**Why:** Mixing unsigned prospects into scheduled work makes the admin list harder to scan and can make leads look like confirmed jobs.

**How to apply:** When changing job-list filters, counts, sort groups, or quote-status controls, keep lead records isolated from signed jobs and ensure non-signed quote filters open the Lead queue rather than producing an empty jobs list.
