---
name: Lead queue separate from jobs
description: UX and filtering rule for the admin jobs list.
---

Keep unsigned leads in a separate, selectable Lead queue when no search is entered. The main jobs list and its type counts should contain signed/confirmed work only; entering the Lead queue should show requests ordered by their request activity date and keep lead-specific quote filters available. An explicit text search is global across jobs and leads, regardless of the selected queue/category/period, and includes client email and phone contacts.

**Why:** Mixing unsigned prospects into scheduled work makes the admin list harder to scan and can make leads look like confirmed jobs. The owner explicitly wants global contact lookup during a search so a prospect can be recovered without recreating them manually.

**How to apply:** Keep ordinary browsing and counts separated, but show both kinds of matching records in explicit search results with recognizable lead labels/grouping. Clearing the search restores the selected queue and filters.
