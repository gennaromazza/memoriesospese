---
name: Bundle photo pools
description: How bundle photo counts relate to the shared shooting gallery and per-product selections.
---

For bundles that include a shooting, the studio prepares/uploads the digital gallery photos. Photo counts on accompanying products (for example, an album) are per-product selection requirements from that same gallery, not additional unique shots to produce. The customer can assign photos to each included product separately; a photo may be reused across products.

**Why:** The studio clarified that a shooting with 40 photos plus an album requiring 10 uses one 40-photo gallery; the album's 10 are not 10 extra photos to shoot.

**How to apply:** Customer-facing bundle summaries should describe the shared gallery size once (use the largest shared photo quota) and label component counts as per-product. Preserve existing per-product photo assignment requirements; do not replace them with a single merged selection.
