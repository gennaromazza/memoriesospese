---
name: Bundle photo pools
description: How bundle photo counts relate to the shared shooting gallery and per-product selections.
---

For a bundle with shared photos enabled, the admin selects the included source product that defines the gallery. Photo counts on accompanying products (for example, an album) are per-product selection requirements from that same gallery, not additional unique shots to produce. The customer can assign photos to each included product separately; a photo may be reused across products. Without an enabled shared-photo source, retain the existing non-shared bundle behavior.

**Why:** The studio clarified that a shooting with 40 photos plus an album requiring 10 uses the shooting's 40-photo gallery; the album's 10 are not 10 extra photos to shoot. The source is chosen explicitly in the bundle editor, rather than inferred from the largest component.

**How to apply:** In the bundle editor, enable shared photos and choose the included source product. Customer-facing summaries should show that source's gallery size once and label component counts as per-product. Preserve existing per-product photo assignment requirements; do not replace them with a single merged selection.
